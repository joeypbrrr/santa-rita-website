# Reference-match page

Older zips are unchanged. This page follows the attached food poster’s composition.

## What matches

The poster is a centered column on a warm orange surround.

1. A dark red opening with a slim bar: the name, the phone, Directions, and a small yellow Call control.
2. A stacked headline on the left: “Santa Rita” in yellow, “Grocery” in cream, a short line, and a red rectangular Call button. Directions sits beside it.
3. The food is in the foreground, not in a frame. The pastry plate stays upper right. The sandwich board sits lower and to the right, so the headline, the address line, and Call / Directions stay on the dark red with no board behind them. The board still crosses the torn edge.
4. The opening meets the cream through a coarse, generated brush edge, not a smooth wave.
5. The cream band is centered: “Come by,” the address line, and a red Call button.
6. A wide gold brush carries the sandwich, the plate, and the tray, overlapping the cream above and below. They are cutouts, not tilted cards.
7. Three light cards with round crops sit on the rough change into the red close.
8. The storefront is in that lower block, next to 1053 Cotton Street, so the building is still identifiable.

The poster’s burgers, pizza, garnish drawings, logo, prices, and Order button are not used.
